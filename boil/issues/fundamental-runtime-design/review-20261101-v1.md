## Findings and dispositions

1. **Region service and observation contract — resolved.** The earlier draft
   described a no-safepoint region without explicitly bounding service of
   backedges/eval breakers or asynchronous observers. Allocation-free execution
   alone cannot justify delayed signals, monitoring activation, queued work or
   visible frame state. The accepted snapshot explicitly requires bounded
   regions, backedge/breaker service and an observer protocol before delayed
   publication. Subsequent source proposals must still name original-word
   error/monitoring states and legal debugger entry; this design grants no
   blanket removal of IP stores.

2. **Source-layer attribution — resolved.** The earlier draft's “current
   candidate” could conflate the maintained evaluator with isolated nursery
   and iterator implementations. The final text names maintained VM source
   `4615e52c9bb`, qualified e9 captures, nursery/cadence `47ccf35e...` and
   unbuilt iterator `c43c8374...`. It explicitly denies a nursery/iterator
   landing implication. Future executable manifests require full identities
   and source/product bridges, as before.

3. **Generational mechanism and external analogy — resolved.** “Requires
   complete core write barriers” was too categorical outside a specified
   remembered-edge policy. The accepted text scopes that requirement to its
   selected design and leaves other tracking mechanisms subject to their own
   proof. StickyImmix's in-place generation policy is also distinguished from
   full-heap movement settings. MMTk's actual source makes `moves_objects`
   conditional on `immix_non_moving`; its name alone is not a stable-address
   proof. [MMTk implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mmtk/mmtk-core/master/src/plan/sticky/immix/global.rs).

4. **Avoid a second ungrounded metadata system — resolved.** The snapshot now
   explicitly starts from the existing allocating/reentrant call effects and
   precise roots, and states that partial H2 is not a complete root-map
   producer. SSA/block parameters and root maps are tools for named consumers,
   not performance conclusions or prerequisites for every small region.

an allocation bit with initialized-object membership.

The rejected slot-base native paths show its intended exit-cache pair removed
and additional common frame/IP memory accesses retained. The candidate

not assigned wholesale to dispatch or allocator overhead. Richards' retained
profile has zero timed GC; this does not generalize to every Richards run.

## Architectural judgment and limits

The ranking is coherent: finish the small object-map hypothesis's missing
failure/recovery proof, then test one effect-complete region, then investigate
one integrated storage cohort and complete old-edge tracking if justified.
Neither SSA, an LLVM backend, an arena allocator nor a write barrier is accepted
as an automatic speedup. Ordinary frame homes remain the baseline; larger
materialization/observer changes require separate design. VM0 remains a
compiler/backend control rather than an architectural endorsement.

The span/arena recommendation is a defensible target constrained by stable
native pointers, lifecycle ordering, raw/opaque owners and measured resource
tradeoffs. It is not validated by the old profiles or external collectors.
Exact coroutine/frame storage remains a candidate whose admission depends on
actual allocation/death evidence. No allocator rewrite or reduced correctness
contract has been authorized.

Each next source grant must freeze one consumer, negative oracle, semantic
boundaries, source/feedback clocks and whole-workload elapsed/resource decision.
The existing failed-prefix cleanup gap is still a real blocker to iterator
feedback completeness; this review does not waive it or resume its held work.
