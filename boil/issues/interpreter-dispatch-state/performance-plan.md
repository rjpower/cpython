## Elapsed acceptance and descriptive noise checks

For each round, report all five durations, median, minimum, maximum and median
absolute deviation (MAD) for both sources, plus candidate/parent median ratio.
Five serial samples are correlated and supply no confidence interval.

The primary coroutine median must improve by at least 1% in **both** rounds.
Additionally, each round's absolute median benefit must exceed the sum of the
two within-cell MADs. Report all ten leave-one-out sensitivity ratios: remove
one duration from one source at a time, leave the other source unchanged, and
recompute its median ratio. Every such primary ratio must remain below 1.
These are predeclared descriptive adequacy checks, not significance claims.
An apparent win that fails either noise check is inconclusive and cannot earn
acceptance from its two point medians. No automatic retiming resolves it.

A greater-than-2% regression repeated in both rounds on generators or either
control needs a bounded causal explanation/correction before acceptance.
Report every row regardless of disposition. Failure of the primary benefit
rule rejects this increment; a new hypothesis needs its own scope. Unexpected
environment, origin, correctness, cleanup or quiet-policy failures stop the
campaign and preserve its partial evidence before diagnosis.
