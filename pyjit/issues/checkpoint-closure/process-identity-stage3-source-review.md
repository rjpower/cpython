The frozen review layers `v1` through `v5` remain available; apply the consolidated packet **instead of** those layers. It changes only the native producer, two child-creation call sites, strict R27 reader and fixtures, and benchmark consumer and fixtures.

The producer records a receipt after a successful `fork`, `fork1`, `forkpty`, `posix_spawn[p]`, or `_posixsubprocess` child creation. Each receipt names the actual child PID and parent interval; a failed publication is retained through a temporary file or sticky dump count. A configured interpreter keeps recording children while stats collection is toggled off. A process that has never allocated stats emits no receipt, so a later `_stats_on` starts its own interval without a false prior failure. The reader requires exact root launches, completed markers and native parent-child receipts; unknown images and incomplete evidence remain gate failures. The benchmark consumer validates its private dump/marker pair and journals both raw artifacts before rejecting malformed evidence.
