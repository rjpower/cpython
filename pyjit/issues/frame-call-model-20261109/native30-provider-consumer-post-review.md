# Native30 provider/consumer post-review

Approved for the bounded engineering commit after reading actual regenerated C,
metadata and the final frameobject consumer. No worker native run or regeneration
was performed.

Independent block comparison confirms only INSTRUMENTED_JUMP_FORWARD,
INSTRUMENTED_JUMP_BACKWARD and INSTRUMENTED_POP_ITER changed. All 262 ordinary
handlers and the earlier ten observed handlers are identical to the committed
baseline; ordinary SHA256 remains

The backward poll precedes observation. Each provider publishes return_pc before
the callback and the selected real PC before deferred retirement. Typed errors
retain the callback exception and latest PC. POP_ITER consumes both input homes,
holds its iterator independently, reports prev_instr separately from its actual
site and declares owner FALLTHROUGH despite public BRANCH_RIGHT. There are no
old stores or iterator closes after dispatch authority changes.

The consumer retains its positive zero-I/O neutral outcome, exact scoped
frame/event/generation/site/return_pc/successor checks. New cancellation is
bounded to inherited context and empty target scratch; strict old FOR/caught
recipes remain available without gaining cancellation permission. Stale scopes
cannot revive old edge facts at a newer landing. Final warning reproof preserves
OTHER refusal and rechecks selected authority.

Coordinator results: core 343 cases with one skip per release/debug, five
generator checks per profile and the corrected twelve-case minimal gate passed.
The first POP_ITER test fixture emitted no POP because direct any(Iterator())
does not meet the compiler's generator-expression optimization. The reviewed
one-expression generator form now proves actual POP_ITER before exercising its
callback error; no check was skipped or weakened. Wider 999-case runs with
four failed modules remain incomplete qualification; trace errors fell from 38
to 24. This review does not claim a full suite pass or final performance gate.
